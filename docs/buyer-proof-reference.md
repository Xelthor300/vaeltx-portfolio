- [Search and filter projects](https://vaeltx-orynt-ai-concept.vercel.app/app/projects): Choose Operations: four projects remain. Try an unmatched search, then clear the filters.

## Vault TCG

A conceptual storefront with query-based search, set and condition filters, product details and a browser-local cart. No inventory, prices or payments are connected.

Case: https://vaeltx-portfolio.vercel.app/work/vault-tcg

Verified technologies: Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Browser local storage, CSS, Vercel.

- [Search and filter the catalog](https://vaeltx-portfolio.vercel.app/concepts/vault-tcg/search): Search a card name. Combine set and condition filters on the catalog to reveal a useful empty state.
- [Choose a product condition](https://vaeltx-portfolio.vercel.app/concepts/vault-tcg/cards/ember-keeper): Choose a condition and add a demo item. Different conditions remain separate cart lines.
- [Review the local cart](https://vaeltx-portfolio.vercel.app/concepts/vault-tcg/cart): Change a line's quantity or remove it, then inspect the non-payment checkout. No purchase is made.

Visual evidence:

- [Comparison starts in the catalog.](https://vaeltx-portfolio.vercel.app/images/proof/vault-catalog-desktop.webp) — 1440 × 900.

Limits: Independent concept. Original fictional cards, no sales or payment processing, no real stock or order creation. This is custom frontend evidence, not proof of Shopify delivery.
