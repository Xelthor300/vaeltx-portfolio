# Visual assets

The visual refinement uses original AI-generated concept images created through the connected Canva account. They do not depict commissioned work, customer properties, physical paintings, licensed trading cards or real inventory. The visible concept disclosures remain part of every project.

| Asset | Source | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `northstar-residence.webp` | [Canva MAHW0ImoR8o](https://www.canva.com/M/MAHW0ImoR8o) | Illustrative property in the Northstar hero |
| `mira-midnight-garden.webp` | [Canva MAHW0fuxB2M](https://www.canva.com/M/MAHW0fuxB2M) | Digital concept study, gallery and artwork viewer |
| `mira-borrowed-light.webp` | [Canva MAHW0XgeBpY](https://www.canva.com/M/MAHW0XgeBpY) | Digital concept study, gallery and product demo |
| `mira-quiet-orchard.webp` | [Canva MAHW0B7Z9fM](https://www.canva.com/M/MAHW0B7Z9fM) | Digital concept study, gallery and artwork viewer |
| `vault-worlds.webp` | [Canva MAHW0a4AP2E](https://www.canva.com/M/MAHW0a4AP2E) | Four original fantasy scenes, presented in fictional card frames |

The browser exported available display-size images, which were encoded as WebP. These are not described as full-resolution source exports. Interface previews in `preview-*.webp` are screenshots of this repository's own concept home pages, captured at 1280 × 800 and encoded at 1200 × 750.

Instrument Sans is self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License bundled in `src/app/fonts/OFL.txt`. Existing original SVG assets remain available in the repository. No commercial image subscription or new paid plan was purchased.
